# Heisman data verification — change log (v2)

All 90 players were searched individually. Sources: each player's English Wikipedia page (infobox + prose), with Pro-Football-Reference / cflapedia as a second check on the doubtful ones (Cassady, C. White, Torretta, Baker, Ware).

## Rules applied
1. A year shows only if the player was on that team that season **and** was not in the military or in a redshirt / sit-out / freshman-squad year.
2. Suspended or injured seasons still count (Hornung 1963, Cappelletti 1979, C. White 1983, Detmer 2000, Weinke 2003–04).
3. Traded midseason → the team they ended the season on. Dropped midseason and a free agent at season's end → the starting team.
4. Retirements, pre-season cuts, camp-only stints and non-playing years with no team are blank.
5. Ignored leagues: XFL, UFL, AAF, FCF. Kept: NFL, AFL, AAFC, WFL, USFL, CFL, NFL Europe.
6. Torretta limited to Vikings 1993 + Seahawks 1996.

## Judgement calls (easy to flip)
- Injured seasons count only with a roster record. Davis 1952 (injured reserve) and Sims 1985 (PUP list) are confirmed and kept. Owens 1975 had no roster record, so it was removed.
- Ware 1994: Wikipedia and the Texas Sports HOF both say he began 1994 with the LA Raiders and was released without playing.
- Janowicz 1956 (crash before the season) and Beban 1970 (cut in preseason) are blank.
- Ricky Williams 2006: shown as Toronto (he played there while suspended from the NFL).
- Vessels 1954–55 are blank (military).

## Pre-publication checks (second pass)
**College seasons re-checked** (Wikipedia infobox + text): Berwanger Chicago 1933–35, Kelley Yale 1934–36, Frank Yale 1935–37, Kinnick Iowa 1937–39, Harmon Michigan 1938–40, Sinkwich Georgia 1940–42, Bertelli Notre Dame 1941–43 (left for the Marines after six games of 1943, so 1943 is kept), Kazmaier Princeton 1949–51, DeVonta Smith Alabama 2017–20, Bryce Young Alabama 2020–22. No changes.
- O'Brien (TCU): infobox says 1936–38, body text says he was Sammy Baugh's backup "in 1935". Kept 1936–38 (Baugh played through 1936; O'Brien started 1937–38).
- Vessels (Oklahoma): Wikipedia infobox says 1949–52, the College Football Hall of Fame says 1950–52. Kept 1950–52 (freshmen were ineligible in 1949).

**Injured seasons**
- Glenn Davis 1952: confirmed. The Rams' 1952 roster (Wikipedia) lists him under Reserve as "Glenn Davis RB (IR)"; he did not play and was released in September 1953.
- Billy Sims 1985: confirmed. Lions 1985 roster lists him on the physically-unable-to-perform list (transactions: PUP 8/20/1985; retired 7/23/1986).
- Steve Owens 1975: not confirmed, so the row was removed from the data. Wikipedia says he "sat out the entire 1975 season" (knee ligament damage from Thanksgiving 1974) and retired in the August 1976 Lions camp. One transaction database lists a Lions "Released" entry in 1975 with no date. No source states his 1975 roster status.

**Draft picks, second source**
- AFL: Baker (San Diego, 12th round, pick 90) and Garrett (Kansas City, pick 178, round 20) confirmed on Wikipedia's 1963 and 1966 AFL draft pages. Cannon, Bellino, Ernie Davis, Staubach and Huarte confirmed on their own Wikipedia pages.
- AAFC: Walker (Cleveland, round 9, pick 69) confirmed on Wikipedia's 1949 AAFC draft page. Blanchard (Brooklyn Dodgers), Glenn Davis (San Francisco 49ers) and Lujack (Chicago Rockets) confirmed as special selections on Wikipedia's 1947 AAFC draft page.
- Not counted: Bellino's 1968 AFL expansion-draft selection by the Bengals, and the USFL drafts.
